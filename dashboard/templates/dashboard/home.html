Air Quality Index

What does AQI mean?

AQI turns a PM2.5 measurement into a health category. A higher number means more particle pollution and a greater chance of health effects.

0–50Good
Air quality conditions may pose health concerns for the general population. Consider reducing prolonged outdoor activities and taking precautions when spending time outside.
51–100Moderate
Air quality conditions are favorable for most individuals. People can safely continue normal activities with minimal concern.
101–150Air quality conditions are generally suitable for most individuals. Sensitive groups may benefit from limiting prolonged exposure to outdoor air.s
Children and people with heart or lung conditions should reduce prolonged exertion.

AirGuard applies AQI health categories to indoor low-cost sensor readings as community guidance. It is not a regulatory outdoor AQI report.
